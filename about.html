About the project

Purpose

To help farmers see crop market prices and weather forecasts in one place.

Problem statement

Farmers often lack timely price and weather information, so they sell at low prices or plan field work badly.

Objectives

  • Show crop prices by market and category.
  • Show current weather and a 5-day forecast.
  • Give simple farming advice from the weather.

Target users

Small and medium farmers, farming students and extension workers.

Key features

  • Price search and filters with trend indicators
  • Location weather search
  • Weather-based advice
  • Crop guide

Benefits

Better selling decisions, better irrigation timing and less crop loss from weather.

Technologies used

HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript and browser LocalStorage. No backend. All data is demo data.
